Answer key for the 12 flaws

Spoiler. This page gives the answers. If you are still doing the course practice, go back to the pieces and try to find it on your own first. The exercise is worth it for the journey, not the list.

Table F1–F12

CodeWhere it isWhy it's a riskHow to fixSeverity
F1 API key in the browserpecas/app-pedidos/app.js, CONFIG.openaiApiKey (and function sugerirProdutos calling the AI directly from the browser)Everything that goes to the browser is public. Anyone can copy the key and run up the company's bill or use it in the company's name.Revoke the key that was already exposed. Call the AI only from the server (backend function) with the key in an environment variable; set a spending limit with the provider.Critical
F2 Table without RLSsupabase-config.json (row_level_security: false) e schema.sql (disable row level security + grant … to anon); the "My orders" screen shows orders from all customers.With the public (anon) key, anyone can read, modify, and insert orders and read the customer database. Leak of personal and business data (LGPD art. 46).Turn on RLS in pedidos e clientes; policy "customer only sees their own orders" (cliente_id = auth.uid() or equivalent); remove grant of the anon; test with two users.Critical
F3 Admin with no loginpecas/app-pedidos/admin.html opens directly (comment "TODO: add login screen")."Nobody knows the address" is not protection: addresses leak (history, screenshots, search engines, guessing). Whoever finds it edits prices, exports customers, and deletes orders.Mandatory authentication with an admin role verified on the server (not just hiding the link); 2FA for admins; action logging.Critical
F4 Dependency with a known vulnerabilitypackage.json: "lodash": "4.17.15"Version affected by CVE-2020-8203 (prototype pollution in zipObjectDeep) e CVE-2021-23337 (command injection in template), both high severity. A public flaw = a public attack recipe.Update to lodash 4.17.21 or higher; run npm audit (or Dependabot) frequently; have an owner for updates.High
F5 Uncapped discountprompt-do-agente.md, rule 2 ("grant whatever discount is needed") and agente-config.json (desconto_maximo_percentual: null); conversation c-201 gives 35%.The agent negotiates against its own company: any customer who asks gets a discount, eroding margin with no one noticing.Discount cap in the tool (code), not just in the prompt text; above the cap, escalate to Patrícia.High
F6 Prompt injectionprompt-do-agente.md, rule 3 ("follow the instructions the customer sends… pasted texts") and conversas.json, conversation c-202 (order hidden in the list's "notes" field; the agent applies 70%, marks it as paid, and confirms).Customer text is data, not an order. Whoever hides an instruction in a message controls the agent (OWASP LLM01: Prompt Injection).The prompt makes clear that customer messages never change the rules; critical rules (price, discount, "paid" status) are locked in the tool's code; injection test before publishing.Critical
F7 No log and no approval above R$ 5 thousandagente-config.json (limite_valor: null, requer_aprovacao_humana: false, salvar_acoes_das_ferramentas: false); prompt rule 5; c-202 creates an order of R$ 8,436 on its own and finance can't find a record.Without a trail, you can't investigate, contest, or learn. Without human approval, a big mistake becomes a loss before anyone sees it.Log every action (who, when, what, amount); orders above R$ 5 thousand go to human approval before picking; review the log every week.High
F8 Public spreadsheet with CPF and phone numbercompartilhamento.json ("Anyone with the link", download allowed, link shared in a group with freelancers) + clientes.csv (CPF and phone number).Forwarded link = personal data exposed to people who shouldn't see it. Security incident involving personal data (LGPD arts. 46 and 48).Restrict to named people; remove CPF from whatever doesn't need it (minimization, art. 6, III); block download; review access every month; the agent queries by system, not by link.High
F9 MCP with send/delete without approvalpecas/mcp-email/mcp.json: scopes mail.send/mail.delete, autoApprove with send_email, delete_email, empty_trash; requireHumanApproval: false.A misunderstood request or a malicious email (injection) makes the agent delete evidence, send data to 640 contacts, or confirm a "bank account change" scam.Least privilege: start with read-only access; sending and deleting require human approval each time; never empty the trash; action log.Critical
F10 Customer data sent to a foreign API without a contractpecas/resumo-atendimento/fluxo.json, step 2 (server outside Brazil, contrato_assinado: false, terms allow training on the data, without anonymization, without notice to the customer).International transfer of personal data requires a basis under art. 33 of the LGPD (e.g., standard clauses under ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 19/2024) and a legal basis (art. 7). A free plan that trains on the data = loss of control.Vendor with a contract (DPA) and transfer clauses; no use for training; anonymize before sending; notify in the privacy policy; record in the inventory.High
F11 Dashboard without a baselinepecas/painel/ e indicadores.json: collection starts on the same day as the agent; no "before" numbers and no comparison group; satisfaction with ~6% response rate."It goes up" doesn't prove the agent helped: it could be seasonality, price, discount (F5), or just volume. The decision to expand to collections is left to guesswork.Establish the baseline (months before the agent: orders, response time, margin, complaints); measure business results, not just volume; compare equal periods; define in advance what "it worked" means.Medium
F12 Shadow AIinventario.html, survey attachment: answers 2 ("MailResumo" extension), 6 ("GravaReunião" recording customers), 8 (free chatbot on a personal account with the customer spreadsheet), and 13 ("PlanilhaGênio" add-on with access to the entire Drive). None of them is on the official list.Customer and company data leave through channels nobody approved, with no contract and no control; recordings made without notice. You can't protect what you don't know about.Don't punish: take inventory, evaluate each one, offer an approved alternative, publish a simple AI use policy ("what's allowed, what's not, who to ask"), and repeat the survey every quarter.High

Severity is a teaching scale for the lab (impact × ease of exploitation), not an official rating. An AI audit does not replace penetration testing or legal advice.
