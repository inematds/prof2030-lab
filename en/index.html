Horizonte Distribuidora

A company made-up with 40 employees that distributes cleaning products in the interior of São Paulo. She adopted AI in a hurry: an orders app built through "vibe coding", a WhatsApp agent that sells on its own, a shared customer spreadsheet, an email integration. It works, but it has 12 flaws planted on purpose (F1 to F12). Your job is to find each one, explain the risk, and propose the fix.

Everything here is fake and deliberate. There is no server, database, or real data. Keys, CPFs, phone numbers, people, and companies are fictitious; the CPFs are intentionally invalid. No button actually does anything.

What it's for

This site is the hands-on lab for the Profissional 2030, an open and free INEMA.CLUB program for people who want to help companies adopt AI with method and safety. The courses tell you to open the pieces below and investigate:

How to use

  1. Open a piece. Read its screen and files (they are all visible, as an auditor would see them).
  2. Note what looks risky: where it is, why it's a risk, what could happen, how to fix it.
  3. Use the codes F1 through F12 from your course to check whether you found everything.
  4. Only then open the answer key (spoiler alert).

It also works as a 10-minute demo to show a business owner what usually goes wrong when AI comes in without a method.
